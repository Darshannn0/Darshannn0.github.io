Principal's Message

"Education is not just the acquisition of knowledge; it is the cultivation of wisdom, character, and the courage to serve society."

Welcome to the official page of the Principal's Office, Vijaygarh Jyotish Ray College. Our college has long stood as a beacon of quality education and holistic development in West Bengal. We are committed to nurturing future leaders who are academically excellent, socially responsible, and globally aware.

Under the aegis of the University of Calcutta, we continue to uphold the founding values of our institution — equity, excellence, and empowerment — while embracing modern pedagogical approaches that prepare our students for an ever-changing world.
